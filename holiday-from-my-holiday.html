I Need a Holiday from My Holiday

Five flights of stairs. A car boot with ambitions of becoming a black hole. Two girls, two dogs, a mountain of luggage — and a holiday that hasn't even started yet.

The story behind the song

Sometimes a holiday isn't a relaxing escape. Sometimes it's an engineering exercise involving luggage Tetris, endless lists and the astonishing question of whether everything will fit into the family car.

Inspired by the chaos of getting the entire household out of the door and into the car, this one takes the stress of a supposedly relaxing break and turns it into a deep house singalong.
