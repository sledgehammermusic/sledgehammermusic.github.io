BEHIND THE STRINGS

About Sledge Hammer Music

Welcome to Sledge Hammer Music — original songs, guitar-driven soundscapes and musical stories inspired by life, dreams, memories and everything in between. Some would call it noise.

Guitarist, songwriter and occasional disturber of the peace. Sledge Hammer is the artist name behind the music.

My music wanders through progressive rock, blues, atmospheric rock, ambient soundscapes, atmospheric deep house and whatever other musical rabbit hole catches my imagination.

I played in bands for more than 20 years, but I no longer have a band — or the budget for session musicians and producers — on standby. AI helps me supply those missing instruments and turn the music in my head into something you can hear.

It usually takes many attempts, rewrites and rejected versions before a song says what I intended. AI provides the tools and virtual musicians; the stories, decisions and heart behind the music remain mine.

It also turns up on time, stays sober and rarely complains about the tempo — although getting it to follow directions is another matter entirely.
