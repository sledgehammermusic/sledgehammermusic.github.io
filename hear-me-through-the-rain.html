Hear Me Through the Rain

She isn't asking for quick answers. She wants someone to see her, listen without judgment, and sit beside her through the night.

The story behind the song

Sometimes the hardest words to say are the ones we most need someone to hear. This song is about feeling invisible even when surrounded by people; about saying “I'm fine” when the truth is more complicated.

It's not a request for advice, or a promise that everything will be all right. It's a request for presence — for someone who can sit through the dark without needing to fix it.

The rain doesn't stop at the end. But there are two steaming mugs on the windowsill. Someone chose to stay.
